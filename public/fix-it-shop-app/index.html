HER BUSINESS. HER STANDARD.

Straightforward, warm, and attentive to the details.

Katie brings technical care and a personal connection to an experience made for men. She is a United States Marine Corps veteran and the founder and owner of Fix It Shop.

The story behind the name

A treasured family book helped inspire the name. Katie preserves her family’s copy of Mr. Bell’s Fixit Shop by Ronne Peltzman. The connection behind it: care for something that matters to someone can reach beyond the thing itself.
