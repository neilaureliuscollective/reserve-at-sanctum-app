# The Arrival — implemented phase-one direction

The Reserve is the shared front door. The page offers a place to belong and an
immediate way to reserve time with Katie. It then opens into two distinct worlds.

## Frame 1: arrive

An obsidian arch catches warm gold light. Through it, a Louisiana live oak anchors
the scene in home. On the left, real accessible type reads **“A higher you belongs
here.”** Booking and exploring are immediately available, with no intro gate.
The architecture is explicitly labeled concept imagery.

## Frame 2: feel the depth

A true 3D Louisiana medallion sits within the architectural scene. Its gold edge
responds subtly to movement and warm light. The background has a gentle opening
drift. Motion is restrained so words and booking remain the focus. The viewer
can pause it; operating-system reduced-motion preference is respected. Without
WebGL, the architecture, message, and links remain usable.

## Frame 3: choose your world

The first scroll reveals two large visual entrances: Fix It Shop in deep blue
and gold; Aurelius Collective in obsidian, purple, and gold. Katie's world feels
personal and attentive. Neil's world introduces purpose, discipline, wellbeing,
and community. The page continues into shared values and Neil + Katie's intent.

Warm serif typography, calm spacing, readable live copy, and clear actions carry
the luxurious feel across booking and account screens. The three service steps
and final review make the practical journey easy to follow on a phone.

This is a design hypothesis, not a claim that every visitor will prefer it.
Before public launch, observe a handful of representative local users finding
Katie's services and completing a test booking on their own phones. Check whether
they understand the two worlds and can explain what the Reserve offers.
