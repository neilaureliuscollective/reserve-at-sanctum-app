# Vitalis — program-specific partner quote request

Prepared October 8, 2026. Draft for founder use; no message has been sent.

Legacy Reserve Vitalis is planning an integrated digital-wellness membership and licensed-partner care experience for eligible adults. Our proposed prices are Essential $49, Optimize $149 and Sovereign $249 monthly. These are positioning targets, not live clinical offers. We need an itemized program-specific quote and written operating responsibilities before deciding whether any core treatment can be included.

Please address the following in a written proposal:

1. **Program and geography:** supported states, age/eligibility requirements, provider availability, testosterone pathways and any supported women’s hormonal care. Identify which additional treatment categories are actually available and appropriate; do not substitute a general marketing list for this program’s scope.
2. **Clinical responsibility:** legal provider entity, licenses, intake and exclusion criteria, synchronous/asynchronous requirements, initial and follow-up cadence, clinical messaging, ongoing monitoring, escalation, after-hours support and continuity when a member cancels.
3. **Provider costs:** initial reviews/live consultations, repeat consultations, follow-up reviews, declined/ineligible applications, missed appointments and cancellation charges. Specify unit, frequency, volume commitments and whether fees apply without a prescription.
4. **Laboratories:** required initial and follow-up panels, interpretation, blood-draw/collection charges, lab network coverage, shipping, repeat testing and responsibility for abnormal results. Distinguish retail and partner prices.
5. **Medication and fulfillment:** program-specific medication price by formulation/dose/package, prescribing constraints, pharmacy/dispensing, supplies, fulfillment, shipping, refills, shortages, refunds and dose-change economics. Clarify whether patient or brand is charged and whether any quote is binding.
6. **Platform and launch:** base license, metered messaging/tools, integration/setup charges, monthly minimums, operating support, service levels, exit/export rights and all third-party fees. Itemize one-time versus recurring costs.
7. **Billing and memberships:** merchant of record for memberships, consultations and medications; approved processors; permitted bundling; invoice disclosures; refunds, chargebacks, cancellation and failed-payment responsibilities. Can a $149 nonmedical membership coexist transparently with separately billed partner care? What approved structure would permit a clinically eligible treatment-inclusive alternative?
8. **Integration:** supported hosted/embedded flows, actual sandbox access, API specification/version, authentication, signed webhook verification, retry/idempotency behavior, operational statuses, cancellation/consent handling and error/escalation pathways. We will not implement undocumented endpoints.
9. **Privacy and contracts:** minimum data required for referral, clinical consent responsibility, applicable contracts/BAAs, retention/deletion, subprocessors, breach response and prohibited secondary uses. Legacy Reserve should not need to retain clinical histories, lab results or prescribing decisions for ordinary membership/retail accounting.
10. **Commercial scenarios:** itemized quotes for 25, 100, 300 and 500 active members, with initial acquisition, follow-up utilization and eligible-treatment assumptions stated. Include a sample monthly bill and a sample patient bill for both treatment-inclusive and separately billed structures where available.

Please confirm the specific legal entities and contracts behind Connect Scripts, the provider network, pharmacy and any Connect Telehealth/Connect-Go CRM services. Public documentation describes capabilities and broad cost ranges; it does not establish our contract, wholesale treatment price or integration entitlement.

No clinical enrollment or charges are authorized by this request. Founder review of the complete offer, contracts and billing arrangement is required before a controlled launch.
