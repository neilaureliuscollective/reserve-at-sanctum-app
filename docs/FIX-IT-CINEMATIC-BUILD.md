# Fix It Shop world — September 26, 2026

## Art and privacy direction

Katie's clients generally do not want their visits posted. The site does not simulate a client portfolio, present synthetic before/after work, or use unapproved testimonials. It shows the setting, anonymous process, and feeling of leaving the chair. All generated scenes are labeled illustrative concept imagery in the interface. They are not photographs of Katie, her clients, her actual work, or the finished facility.

The brand world uses midnight blue, stone, restrained warm gold, and the supplied Fix It Shop emblem. It shares a threshold with The Reserve but has its own personal pace. Spatial continuity, timed light, scroll-held process scenes, and a useful preference preview carry the advanced quality.

## Story sequence

1. Empty chair and invitation, with the genuine supplied emblem, men’s salon positioning and booking path.
2. Arrival and consultation: the visitor's desired direction is part of the service.
3. Craft detail: hair and hands illustrate care without identifiable clients or a claim about Katie's actual work.
4. Departure and continuity: anonymous rear view and copy about a look that works beyond the appointment.
5. The Chair: an explicitly unsaved preference preview leads to the existing consent-based experience.
6. Visit: the booking system is linked, with its illustrative menu and prices disclosed.
7. Discretion: the page explains why it uses concept imagery instead of a client gallery.

The process scenes form one sticky section when motion is enabled. A passive scroll listener feeds one animation frame and bounded opacity and transform values. Still mode and OS reduced motion return a linear sequence where every chapter and link is visible. No WebGL or additional animation dependency is used.

## Asset provenance

Three project-bound WebP concept plates live under public/images/katie. private-chair.webp depicts an empty imagined suite; craft-close.webp depicts an anonymous rear-view haircut process; departure.webp depicts an anonymous rear-view exit moment. The original generated PNGs were converted to WebP. The prior concept suite public/images/fix-it.webp remains in the visit panel. The authentic Fix It emblem is the transparent cutout at public/images/cinematic/fix-it-seal.webp.

Built-in imagegen prompts requested photorealistic editorial realism, midnight navy and warm brass, privacy-safe framing, no identifiable clients, no text or fake logos, no actual Katie likeness, and no barbershop motifs. These plates are concept imagery, not proof of completed services.
