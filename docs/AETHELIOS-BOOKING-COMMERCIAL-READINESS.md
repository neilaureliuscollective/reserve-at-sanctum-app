# Minimal paid Aethelios Booking offering — decision research

October 12, 2026. Proposal only; no subscription integration, prices, charges,
trial accounts, affiliate payouts or marketing automation are activated.

Start with one founder-onboarded independent professional, one approved business,
one location and the existing booking/Studio experience. Include service/hours
configuration, customer scheduling/history, staff appointments/blocks, secure
changes and reliable transactional confirmations/reminders. Sell dependable
operations and personal onboarding, not a replacement POS, generic CRM, AI phone
agent or national marketplace. Katie's verified pilot and business-isolation
foundation precede any promise to a second independent business.

## Pricing and trial hypothesis

Current public US references retrieved for this review:
[GlossGenius](https://glossgenius.com/pricing) lists Standard $28/month and Gold
$56/month, with lower annual equivalents; those plans include broader bundled
features than this first Booking offering.
[Square Appointments](https://squareup.com/us/en/appointments/pricing) is a
separate payment-centered competitor; plan/location/payment terms should be
rechecked when approving a price. Do not infer a payment cost advantage from a
software-only fee or mix Square processing with Shopify product commerce.

Founder decision hypothesis: test **$29–39/month per single-professional business**,
with a published founding-business rate for a defined term, not an unlimited
lifetime discount. This is an unapproved hypothesis, not a configured price.
Validate willingness to pay and the support burden with Katie and a small,
personally recruited cohort after pilot acceptance. Avoid annual prepayment until
retention and recovery are demonstrated.

Offer a 30-day founder-assisted trial starting only after configuration and pilot
verification. No card required during this first controlled trial, no automatic
charge and no public sign-up flow. Define included onboarding time, message
allowance and fair-use limits before offering an unlimited plan.

## Owner lifecycle and support

The future lifecycle is approved prospect → verified owner membership → configured
business → isolated rehearsal → pilot verified → founder-approved activation →
trial → paid → paused/cancelled → export/retention/deletion under reviewed policy.
Commercial entitlement must be a separate subscription state, never a global
user-role promotion. A failed payment must not delete appointments or silently
prevent customers cancelling/rescheduling. Owner transfer and membership
revocation need auditable expected revisions and verified identities.

Before charging: establish support hours and incident ownership, delivery-failure
triage, restore rehearsal, outage/fallback playbook, customer/business export and
retention rules, written software terms/privacy, cancellation and refund policy,
and approved server-side subscription lifecycle handling. Measure real support
minutes, transactional sends, hosting/database/backup cost and payment-subscription
fees; do not project fictional revenue or profit.

A future subscription provider integration should handle signed receipts,
idempotent/out-of-order updates, entitlement reconciliation, dunning and owner
self-service cancellation in a dedicated reviewed phase. It must not take over
Square service payments or Shopify product checkout. No processor account,
product, price or checkout session is created in this increment.

Optional Legacy Reserve affiliate commerce is later opt-in, with business consent,
clear disclosure, attributable product orders, explicit commission/refund rules
and payout reconciliation. It must not read Chair data or divert Katie's service
payments. No commerce UI, commission ledger or payout architecture is needed now.

Next paid-platform milestones: (1) Katie hosted pilot and support rehearsal;
(2) independently verified business memberships and isolation; (3) one second
founder-onboarded pilot; (4) approve offering/terms/cost limits; (5) subscription
lifecycle implementation and synthetic processor acceptance; (6) separately
approve live billing. Marketing and affiliate work follow demonstrated demand.
