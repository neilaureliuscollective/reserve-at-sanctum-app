Public reference retrieval was attempted for the following pages, including an escalated read, but the environment proxy returned HTTP 403. These are supporting references, not newly retrieved research or evidence of measured user outcomes:

- W3C contrast guidance, https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html — verify normal text at 4.5:1, large text at 3:1; interactive boundaries/focus at 3:1 where required.
- web.dev animation guidance, https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide — prefer transform/opacity; honor reduced motion and retain static content.
- Existing repository research on Apple materials and Aman architectural composition informs distinct readable planes, negative space and restrained surface treatment. This is design interpretation, not a fresh audit of those sites.

## Plan and implementation

1. Dark emerald public navigation and a full-width architectural hero. Ivory typography, champagne emphasis, readable opaque gradient over illustrative imagery, account/collection actions and an immediate direction anchor. No imagery or product claims invented.
2. Compact emerald standards strip, followed by the useful digital ecosystem before products. Blue belongs to this focused tools chapter; selecting Presence, Performance or Vitalis changes the ring geometry, icon, color, explanation and real destination in the same panel. On phones, a selection brings the controls and result into view when the panel extends below the viewport.
3. One mineral-ivory collection chapter with a signature product stage. Existing previous-packaging disclosure is retained. Other concepts use dark display stages and compact image/text rows on phones, reducing repeated tall product blocks without hiding products in a carousel.
4. Founder, wellness and membership chapters retain their existing real routes and honest unavailable/planned capabilities. Founder copy is shorter; the founder portrait remains labeled as AI-recreated. Footer returns to deep emerald.
5. Native scroll-linked image camera movement is progressive enhancement. Static fallback, manual still mode, reduced motion, keyboard focus, native no-JavaScript menu/direct direction links, forced colors and reduced transparency are retained.

Palette roles: midnight emerald #0D211B / imperial emerald #12382D, obsidian #080D0B, warm ivory #F2EEE4, blue #123B4E / night #0B202B, Reserve Gold #C4912F borders and champagne #E4BD70 accents. The proposed 60/25/10/5 mix is an art-direction heuristic, not a measured area requirement; photography, viewport and content alter actual coverage. Contrast and clarity take precedence over a percentage.

Changes are local to flagship components and their CSS module. No global palette, auth, booking authority, private data, paid integration, dependency or schema changes.
