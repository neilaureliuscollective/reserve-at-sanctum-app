# Reserve homepage continuation

The September 23 build follows the approved full-page visual reference and its
Louisiana grooming triptych. The entrance now leads with “Small-town roots. A
bigger standard.” The page then moves through the place, Katie and Neil's
distinct strengths, their shared Reserve, the two existing brand worlds, the
continuing care journeys, Eunice, and three explicit routes to begin.

The visual reference is a direction, not a screenshot embedded into the site.
Content is native HTML with keyboard links, responsive CSS, and server-rendered
copy. The existing hero's pause and reduced-motion behavior remain intact.
Concept scenes are identified as such; no illustrated people are represented
as Katie, Neil, customers, or actual visitors. Community and wellness are
stated as a vision rather than live services. All CTAs point to existing routes
or anchors, and the preview's booking and account boundaries are unchanged.

The compressed image is `public/images/reserve-grooming-concept.webp`, from the
approved “Small-town Louisiana grooming triptych.png”. The old architecture
image remains available to other pages. The former simple editorial homepage
is replaced here; this is not a second application.

The three original official logos from the September 22 message are not
recoverable as image files in this checkout or the project Library folder.
The existing GENT Ascend emblem remains in its world. The Reserve's current
header mark and Fix It Shop word treatment remain provisional until the exact
official image assets are available for faithful integration.
